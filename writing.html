Writing

Short stories and paintings

Réflexions à travers deux courts récits et des peintures

A book in French, published as a Kindle edition on Amazon. It pairs two short stories with a selection of my paintings.

“Divin”
A woman meets a soldier created by artificial intelligence, and the story questions the boundary between humans and machines.
“Morgan, la fille de la mer”
A story about immigration and the meeting of cultures.

Together with the paintings, the book explores the wounds of war, exile, memory, resilience, hope and human diversity.
