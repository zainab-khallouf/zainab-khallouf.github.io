Research

Artificial intelligence and cybersecurity

Research interests

My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. My earlier work covered multicast routing security, forward error correction, intrusion detection and the security of web services; my recent work applies neuro-symbolic AI to security problems.

  • Neuro-symbolic AI
  • Logic Tensor Networks
  • Explainable AI
  • Blockchain fraud detection
  • Anomaly detection
  • Network security
  • Distributed systems

Recent work

Neuro-symbolic AI for fraud detection on Ethereum. With P.-F. Marteau (IRISA), we formulated fraud detection as a binary classification task with Logic Tensor Networks and proposed an ensemble framework based on voting. It outperforms about 80% of the individual models on a public Ethereum fraud dataset and fits Ethereum’s peer-to-peer architecture. Presented at the 8th International Conference on Machine Learning for Networking (MLN 2025), Paris.

Earlier work

My Ph.D. (INPG, 2006, CIFRE grant with France Télécom R&D and INRIA Rhône-Alpes) was on the security of multicast routing infrastructures, from the network operator’s point of view. At the University of Homs I worked on intrusion detection, trust management in peer-to-peer SIP, routing in mobile ad hoc networks, the performance of e-commerce applications, and the security of RESTful web services. I supervised 8 master’s theses there.
