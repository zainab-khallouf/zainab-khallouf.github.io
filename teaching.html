Teaching

Cybersecurity, distributed systems, AI and software engineering

I have taught in higher education since 1998: about 1,700 tutorial-equivalent hours (HETD) at ENSIBS, Université Bretagne Sud, since 2021, and more than 3,400 hours at the University of Homs between 2006 and 2021, in Arabic, French and English.

ENSIBS – Université Bretagne Sud (2021–present)

  • Web application security
  • Blockchain and Web3
  • AI and cybersecurity (statistical and neuro-symbolic)
  • Behavioral analysis
  • Distributed and massively parallel computing
  • Secure distributed systems development
  • Component-based software engineering
  • Research methods and technology watch
  • Random number generators; web programming; introductory computing

University of Homs, Syria (2006–2021)

  • Computer network security
  • Information security (undergraduate and Master 1)
  • E-commerce systems
  • Advanced network management and design
  • Wireless networks
  • Distributed systems; operating systems
  • Reliability of computer systems; computer ethics
  • Master’s course, University of Damascus (2008–2010)

Teaching materials

I write my own teaching materials, including lab sessions on CUDA, Numba, gRPC, MPI and Kafka, a PyTorch lab for neuro-symbolic AI, and a bank of more than 100 web-security quiz questions. I am also the pedagogical coordinator of the fourth year of the Cybersecurity and Data Science program at ENSIBS.
